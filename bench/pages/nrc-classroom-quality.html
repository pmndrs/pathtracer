Patch flicker is the 99th percentile of absolute changes in mean display luminance across 8 × 8 render-pixel patches and eight successive frame pairs. Error is mean absolute display-luminance error against a separate 256-spp plain path trace at render resolution. Both measures run from 0 to 1. Median frame time covers the whole rendering chain and excludes readback.
